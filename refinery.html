Development Roadmap

Phase 1: Q4 2023

Conceptual Design & Licensing

Completion of Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) and securing all necessary regulatory permits from the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority (NMDPRA).

Phase 2: Q2 2024

Groundbreaking & Infrastructure

Mobilization to site in Lagos State. Commencement of heavy-duty piling, foundation works for storage terminals, and marine jetty construction.

Phase 3: 2025 - 2026

Mechanical Completion

Installation of modular refining units, catalytic reformers, and full integration of automation systems for 24/7 autonomous operations.
